Frequently Asked Questions

How often should my pet be groomed?

Most dogs and cats do best on a 4–6 week schedule. Doodles, long-coated breeds, and heavy shedders often need more frequent grooming to prevent matting and buildup.

Do you groom both dogs and cats?

Yes. We provide mobile grooming for both dogs and cats, from small breeds to giant breeds and many feline personalities.

What vaccinations are required?

For everyone’s safety, pets should be current on rabies and core vaccines according to your veterinarian’s recommendations.

Where do you park the grooming van?

We typically park in your driveway or directly in front of your home, where we can connect safely and keep your pet close.

How do I pay?

Payment is due at the end of the appointment. We’ll go over accepted payment methods when you book.

Do you offer de-shedding or extra services?

Yes! We offer add-ons such as de-shedding treatment, teeth & fresh breath care, and flea & tick baths (price varies by size).
