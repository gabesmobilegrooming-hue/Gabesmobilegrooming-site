How Gabe’s Mobile Grooming Works

Stress-free grooming at your door

We Come to You

On the day of your appointment, we park right in front of your home. Our grooming van is fully equipped with professional tools, warm water, and climate control—your pet enjoys a private, one-on-one spa experience.

  • No driving across town or waiting in a lobby.
  • Your pet never leaves your property except to step into our van.
  • Perfect for anxious, senior, or multi-dog households.

Cage-Free, Start-to-Finish Grooming

Each pet is groomed individually from start to finish with gentle handling and patience. We focus on making grooming a calm, positive experience.

  • No cages or crowded kennels.
  • Most full grooms average 60–90 minutes, depending on size and coat.
  • We recommend a 4-week schedule to keep coats healthy and tangle-free.

Common Questions

New to mobile grooming? Start here.
How often should my pet be groomed? Most dogs and cats do best on a 4–8 week schedule, depending on their coat. Doodles and long-haired breeds typically need grooming every 4 weeks to prevent matting.
Do you use cages? No. All grooming is one-on-one inside the mobile salon. Your pet goes straight from your home into the van and back again—no cages or crowded kennels.
How do I get a quote? Text (734) 695-9481 with your pet’s name, breed, approximate weight, coat length, and your city. We’ll respond with a starting estimate and available dates.
